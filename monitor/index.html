Where each market sits right now.

A hidden Markov model, refit out of sample on every market below, sorts each day into three volatility states. This panel reports which one each market is in today, how long it has been there, and what that regime's measured volatility implies for position size. It reads risk, not direction.

Method

Three-state Gaussian HMM on seven causal features per market (log-return momentum over 5/21/63/126 sessions, realized volatility over 5/21/63). Fitted with an expanding walk-forward: every fold standardizes on its training rows only, refits the model, and decodes the test block with an online forward filter, so no label ever uses a future observation. Each market is fitted independently, so "Crisis" means crisis for that market rather than one global state pasted across eleven rows. States are ordered by trailing volatility, and the label shown is the newest completed session.

Implied size is target volatility divided by the volatility measured in that regime, capped at 1.0. It is arithmetic on a measured quantity, shown so a risk budget can be read off the regime. It is not a backtested strategy, not a traded position, and not advice. The allocation rule built on these states is shown losing, honestly, in the research write-up.
